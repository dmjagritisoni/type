Grih Mantralaya/Ministry of Home Affairs

No.A-43020/ 12/2017-Ad.I New Delhi, 1st February 2017

ORDER


The sanction of the competent authority is hereby accorded for assignment of consultancy to Shri S.B. Navrang s/o Shri R B P Gupta r/o H.No. F2, Plot 28, Vidhayak Colony, Nyay Khand-I, Indirapura, Ghaziabad, U.P. for handling the work relating to monitoring of social media in the Ministry of Home Affairs with effect from 1st February 2017 for a period of one year or till further orders whichever is earlier. In addition, he will also be responsible for analysis etc. of the social media related issues

The terms and conditions for the assignment of consultancy will be as

under:-

  1. He will be responsible for handling the work of social media i.e.
    Monotoring the activities, analysis and submission of report etc.
  2. The assignment will be part of the Social Media Cell of the Ministry of
    Home Affairs and shall work under overall supervision and control of Additional Director General(Media) of this Ministry.
  3. The Ministry of Home Affairs will pay consolidated amount of
    Rs.70,000/- per month.
  4. He will be entitled to avail Casual Leave at the rate of 8 days in a
    calendar year on pro-rata basis.
  5. The Ministry of Home Affairs reserves the right to terminate the
    assignment at any time, without assigning any reasons therefore.

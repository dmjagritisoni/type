OFFICE MEMORANDUM

The undersigned is directed to say that a conference of all the FAs with Finance Secretary was held in Ministry of Finance on 3rd June 2016. It was decided interalia that a list of employees retiring within one year should be prepared in advance and their retirement formalities should be completed well in time.

Instructions issued by Department of Pension and Pensioners Welfare from time to time should also be strictly adhere to.
